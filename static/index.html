How to use

  1. Get a transcript. Click Record before the meeting starts (it captures your mic and, on Windows, your computer's audio, so online calls work). Or upload a recording, or paste a transcript from Teams/Zoom.
  2. Add context (optional): who's there, names and jargon. This fixes most misheard words.
  3. Ask for what you want, or click a preset. You can ask mid-meeting; new transcript text is sent automatically with each request.
  4. Refine with follow-ups ("shorter", "for Discord", "no emojis").

Everything is saved in the meetings folder: the audio, transcript.txt, and notes.md with every response. Reopen a meeting from Past meetings.

Say "off the record" in the meeting and that part will be left out of the notes. Always get consent before recording people.
